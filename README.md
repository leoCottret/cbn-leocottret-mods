# Goodies for Cataclysm: Bright Night
A collection of mods/tools for [Bright Night](https://github.com/cataclysmbn/Cataclysm-BN).

## Mods
- [XE038](/MODS/XE038). Add late game monsters to challenge your demi-god cyber mutant, big bundle of materials and late game items (_PS: was known as Sudo's requiem_)
- [The Milky Way](/MODS/The_Milky_Way). Add Prolactin Amplification to the Cattle mutation branch, allowing you to milk NPCs once a day with a bucket in your inventory
- (obsolete) XL armors ->  **no need for that now, you can resize most armors with a tailor kit or an arc welder, you can even create a hole for your tail in power armors.**

## Musics
- [Royalty free musics](/MUSICS) that you can add to your favorite soundpack

## Dev tools
- [A dev tool](/DEV_TOOLS/XL_ARMORS_GENERATOR) to generate your own version of XL armors, with custom parameters, for Bright Night and its compatible mods
  - For "superusers" and modders that want to show their love to their fellow mutants
## Game tools
- [A game tool](/GAME_TOOLS/VEHICLE_CREATION_HELPER), using a combination of text recognition and keys stimulation to automatically build your vehicle in game. Huge help for big vehicles
  - Python script (cross-plateform) version available
